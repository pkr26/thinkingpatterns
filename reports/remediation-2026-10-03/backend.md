## Atomic patient rotation contract

`POST /processing/rekey` requires a saved UUID `operation_id`, `new_salt`, `new_verifier`, and exact saved `consent_wraps` carrying `consent_id`, `therapist_wrap_pub_key`, `ephemeral_pub`, `wrapped_key`. V2 also supplies `new_wrapped_data_key` and `new_kdf_params`. The old account verifier and two processing tokens authorize first/resume attempts. Response fields include `credential_rotated`, `operation_id`, `consents_rewrapped` (actual active count), per-store counts and `recovery_invalidated`.

An exact last committed operation may retry using the immediately preceding signed epoch before expired processing tokens are consumed; another body/operation or later epoch invalidation fails. Pending journals bind the body and both key fingerprints. Clients persist the exact operation including random grant wraps, stage local rekey material, resume rather than discarding an interrupted checkpoint, and log in with the new credentials after commit. No separate credential-only swap follows finalization.
